The following is the lore for a browser based strategy video game named Silicon Dawn, released in 2010. The setting takes inspiration from Halo, Battlestar Galactica, Mass Effect, the Freespace games, and the Foundation series of novels.

HISTORY
----------

In 1989, Tiananmen Square was not the site of peaceful protests - it was the beginning of the Blue Revolution. With China turned into a radical student West-leaning government practically overnight, the USSR had an excuse to overthrow glasnost and declare Gorbachev a traitor to the cause. An even stronger police state USSR was able to keep its populace in check and countermand the growing unrest with batons and bullets. 

The Cold War re-escalated, with new and viable missile defense on both sides erasing the very real threat of nuclear Armageddon and allowing the prospect of open warfare. The US and USSR, entrenched even deeper in the hostile mindset of conflicting ideology, ignited countless brushfire wars over the next decades for control of cities as far-flung as Cairo and Brasilia. 

The rift between East and West grew as Communist takeovers of mainland Europe balanced out the West's gain of Blue China. The wave of red, fed by both voluntary and resentful European parties, stopped at the English Channel as US forces crushed the Soviet advance and denied their ambition for a red Europe in 2006. Despite the lives lost, the balance of power remained firmly locked with missile defense satellites overhead denying both sides the ultimate strategic advantage.

Locked in perpetual stalemate, the superpowers took to the heavenly bodies in Sol. A desperate struggle to tip the balance of power one way or the other resulted in decades of competition and countless miles of expansion - everything from Luna to the Kupier Belt fell under the iron grip of one war-torn human power or the other. Ever-increasing feats of nationalism stirred up fervor on both sides, and fueled the expansion. The first Soviet expedition to the moon in 2012 featured the destruction of Apollo 11's flag on its itinerary. The first men to step on the surface of Mars in 2024 did so in USAF combat armor and were killed in action days later.

On Earth, the international political landscape had become unrecognizable. The United Nations “filled in the cracks” left between the conflicting superpowers and became a formidable political force. The Johannesburg and Nairobi Conventions amended Chapter V of the UN Charter to remove veto power and reactivated the United Nations Emergency Force (UNEF) in 1999. As the US and USSR boycotted the UN over the changes and forced their satellites to do the same, the UN”s center of power shifted to Africa, Asia, and Oceania. 

The UN -the UNEF, in particular- were regarded as enemies to both sides - first to the USSR as the United Nations Emergency Force fought bitterly in defense of Western Europe from 2002 to the end of the Channel War in 2006, then by the United States as conflicts between North African US satellite states and sub-Saharan UN nations broke into open warfare for eleven months in 2045.

Increased defense funding on both sides led to development of increasingly unique military technologies. The long-proposed weapon of the magnetically driven railgun became a reality in 2048 when the US Army adopted them for mainstream use. They were quickly proven superior against Soviet-sphere gas-driven rifles. The first widespread harnessing of antimatter began across the breadth of the twenty-first century, as US and USSR vessels spread across Sol.

The arguable greatest advancement of all, however, was the creation of the Turing program - later named the VI. The first Turing program, Ulysses, stunned the world in 2050 as the first computer program capable of passing the Turing test in any instance. This 'personable' program, and the logic therein, was quickly employed in commercial applications worldwide. American radio and network espionage also received a massive boost to efficiency with Turing programs able to 'run interference' for agents as false identities or useful network doubles. By 2060, an estimated 21.6 million Turing programs were active on the greater Internet.

With almost a century gone by and an entire solar system war-torn, the US and USSR were unrecognizable from their states in 1989. Populations worked to the bone and burning with revolutionary anger in the Soviet Union surged against the system in revolt after revolt, while a war-weary America pushed their government to end the war by any means. 

The chance to do so came when Russian Resistance leaders finally made untapped contact with US commanders in 2071 and coordinated a strategy to make full use of American strategic defense and internal unrest in the USSR. The campaign, dubbed Operation Mjolnir in a parody of the communist hammer and sickle, also added the United Nations Emergency Force as a last-minute partner. On June 6, 2071, American forces swept through Western Europe from Britain and north through the Caspian Sea region by UNEF forces. 

Combined with a simultaneous armed uprising in Moscow and its surrounding districts, Mjolnir was a resounding success. By the end of June, the USSR had expended ninety percent of its strategic arsenal -fruitlessly, with USAF strategic defense satellites overhead- and was reduced to a government-in-exile in northern Siberia. American leadership waited until July 4 to accept Soviet surrender, making the day both Independence Day and Victory in Russia Day.

With the fall of the generations-old Soviet Union, the stellar political landscape quickly became fragmented and confusing. Some former Soviet colonies across Sol fell under the control of the new Russian Federation, while others paid for their independence in blood. The United States, pushed by a people weary of war, made no attempt to unify or pacify the various solar regimes. On Earth, a liberated European continent began to pick up the pieces accompanied by a new xenophobic attitude from decades of Soviet oppression.

The creative brilliance of the Turing program's development in 2050 was further built upon by the development of the Virtual Intelligence in 2078. Driven by the massive economic boom following the USSR's collapse in 2071, a group of network developers took the personable but weak Turing program structure and gave it powerful core logic and a state-of-the-art toolset to give it purpose in almost any network application. The resulting Virtual Intelligence was not sentient, but close. It was able to convincingly mimic a human being online, had motive and drive for self-preservation, and could be loaded with any set of network tools to perform tasks from server maintenance and content aggregation to security to network cracking.

The VI's development gave rise to a new generation of 'smart,' infinitely interchangeable quasi-intelligences. They were immediately put to use in almost every aspect of the civilian sector, and field tests on UAVs, UCAVs, and mainline military vessels were quickly forthcoming. The network developers responsible for the VI's rise were quickly billionaires, and numbers of Internet VIs almost rivaled those of human users.

During the 2080s, as America and the free world celebrated their first peace in decades, Europe became tightly knit through overlapping defense treaties. By 2082, the European Union had unified the continent as a political and military authority, solidifying the scattered powers into a militaristic federation. Unlike Unions that might have been, however, this European Union was decidedly hostile towards all outside powers. The EU, unified against the United Nations they believed had ignored them and locked them out of international communication, made ever increasing overtures of hostility in Northern Africa. By 2084, war was inevitable.

The United Nations Emergency Force again mobilized in Northern Africa, seeing heavy action against the European Union Continental Militia in the Trans-Continental War. It was not until UNEF peacekeepers had fought EUCM forces to a standstill outside Cairo in 2084 that the European Parliament agreed to end the war they had started. Hostile attitudes between the UN and EU were to remain, however, until the conclusion of the international rivalry forty years later.

As soldiers of the UNEF and EUCM fought around the sandblasted cities of Northern Africa, the catalyst to the greatest war in Sol was brought into being. Those ex-Soviet colonies that had earned their independence from the new and weak Russian Federation were either Soviet hardliners or revolutionaries, and neither group looked kindly on the Earthbound nations that denied them aid and peaceful diplomacy.

In 2084, 216 of these dissatisfied colonies on dwarf planets, planetoids, and asteroids formed a loose confederation, bound by the revolutionary document The Argument for Liberatio Astrum, more commonly known as just Liberatio Astrum. The Confederacy of Sol, the first wholly off-world government, was born. As of 2086, currency had been standardized, trade flourished, and the Confederacy's growing skirmishes with the US, UNEF, and other Earth national powers in various orbits prompted the creation of the Solar Defense Command - SOLCOM.

SOLCOM soon became a haven for the most hardcore anti-Earth sentiment in the Confederacy. Its overly aggressive policies towards Earth colonies and civilians, culminating in the US Phobos City Massacre and the UN Makemake Incident in 2088, brought global attention to the Confederacy and its military of hardliners. In May of 2088, the United States issued a unilateral declaration of war against the Confederacy of Sol - a rare feat in a postmodern world of police actions and incidents.

The Inter-Solar War was to last 24 years. Both American and Confederate forces were new to wholly orbital conflict, and for most of the war were locked in a low-intensity conflict of guerilla action. Casualties ran high abroad. From Luna to the Kupier Belt, millions of colonists and soldiers were killed - more by the harsh vacuum of space than in battle.

As the 21st century drew to a close, the Inter-Solar War seemed to drag on without end. Daily casualty reports stretched into the thousands as endless lines of colonist body bags were drawn across Earthbound news cameras. The war quickly became unpopular. Despite the American victory over the USSR, the war against SOLCOM failed to rally the American war spirit, resulting in an unpopular war not unlike Vietnam. New political attitudes would rise with the 22nd century, and with them new unity.

With the proverbial changing of the guard as 2099 turned into 2100, the beliefs and votes of many were changing as well. Anti-war liberalism, for better or worse, began to sweep through America and the greater West after being dampened by Cold War mentalities for decades. On Earth, political views were changing in light of what was being dubbed “The Unending War.” Many nations were pressured to abandon colonies in Sol outright, while US and UN colonies received draw-down orders in population capacity. An unanticipated “spirit of unity”� swept through earthbound nations, resulting in several international unions.

The North American Federation, consisting of the United States, Canada, and Mexico, integrated in 2102 in a manner similar to the European Union, with the amero as its currency. Free trade between the three nations helped each economy flourish and bring about the most prosperous segment of the Inter-Solar War. Immediately after the Pan-American Constitution was brought into force, the NAF re-declared its commitment to ending the threat of the Confederacy of Sol. The United Nations, similarly, agreed to commit the UNEF against the Confederacy after negotiations with the United States.

In space, a Confederacy slowly losing a war of attrition turned SOLCOM towards more desperate tactics. Highly classified research for a relativistic weapon against Earth created a breakthrough in relativistic propulsion - vessels that could travel significant fractions of the speed of light. While the R-bomb plan ultimately rejected by the Confederacy Congress as nothing less than genocide, the secret was damning to public opinion of the SOLCOM once uncovered. This discovery, however, would later become crucial to the survival of SOLCOM, even as the member states of the Confederacy crumbled around them.

As the Inter-Solar War moved past its second decade of open warfare, the Confederacy hit the breaking point. One by one, its solar colonies surrendered or were taken by force. In 2112, the Confederacy of Sol formally disbanded and agreed to terms of surrender. Resistance was dealt with harshly, especially by American military forces. An alleged US defense policy to kill ten CS civilians for every US civilian murdered remained the stuff of legend for centuries, and former colonies like Titan and Haumea remained empty for decades after the war. The Confederacy’s former colonies were split between America, Canada, Mexico, and UN member states.

The anti-Earth hardliners in SOLCOM, however, had not yet given up. Relativistic technology discovered during the darkest days of the war enabled them to flee to Gliese 581 and colonize planets c and d there. Due to the slower-than-light nature of the travel, they would not arrive in Gliese 581 and successfully colonize until 2130.

The VI's inherent abilities as a powerful toolset with a convincing personality were enhanced by the study of neural static - the phenomenon that gave human beings creativity. By replicating this static with a close simulation inside VI core logic, a Department of Defense group was able to bring the sixty year old design to its non-sentient limits. The Creative VI, or CVI for short, was released for wartime use against SOLCOM in 2110. The quick end of the war two years later, however, limited its use to trials and field testing.

The DoD released the CVI to a civilian contractor after the war, hoping that the design would stimulate economic growth after the draining Inter-Solar War. The effects of the CVI on the global economy were unforeseen. Economic analyst Douglas Ryans famously stated that “as of 2112, there is no job a human can do that a CVI cannot,” and many believed there was truth to his statement. Corporate layoffs doubled and tripled in the following years as CVIs replaced human employees in many areas of industry. The NAF economy suffered.

On Earth, meanwhile, only two years elapsed until nations again found themselves at war. With the UN’s force projection weakened by the war and internal strife, the NAF and EU were the most dominant powers on Earth in 2116. The Circum-Atlantic War that resulted from tensions between the two that same year was primarily a naval war, with virtually no territory being taken by either side. 

Meanwhile, desperate to stop the unintended cataclysmic effect of the CVI on the NAF economy, the North American Federation banned the use of them under guise of wartime security. In American, Canadian, and Mexican military forces, however, the CVI flourished as pilots and main computers for untethered UAV, UCAV, and orbital defense. The European Union found itself unable to resist waves of expendable CVI-driven drone forces, leading partially to the destructive bombardment of European cities only a year later. The heavy bombardment of European cities by NAF member navies resulted in a ceasefire by 2118 and the dissolution of the European Union altogether by 2120. 

With the European nations no longer a united power, and dissuaded from doing so again by strong North American Federation military power across the globe, a power vacuum in the global political structure became apparent. In 2124, the United States, Canada, and Mexico dissolved the North American Federation, realizing that their differences were greater than previously anticipated. By 2135, the global political map of Earth was almost entirely unchanged from 2071.

That would soon change. The Chinese Republic, which had in the intervening period moved away from traditional ‘East’ and ‘West’ politics following the collapse of the USSR, began to asset the considerable military and political power that its billion-man population suggested. It reconciled with Taiwan and re-integrated the island in 2126, deployed newfound naval power across the Pacific Ocean in blue-water capacity, and entered into several exclusive-defense agreements with nations such as Indonesia and Vietnam. 

Fearing that China was building up its own political trans-national bloc and threatening American naval power in the Pacific, the US increased naval power in the region. China countered, and between 2126 and 2131 became increasingly hostile towards each other. In November 2132, a mere two years after the forgotten SOLCOM hardliners created their first colony in Gliese 581, the Sino-American War was underway. The war only lasted five months, ending abruptly with a ceasefire in 2133 soon after pre-war tensions gave way to two sides less willing to commit to open warfare than previously anticipated. 

While the powers of the world sought to cut short China's growing hegemony, the American government took endless criticism for its conduct of the Sino-American war. By 2140, American politicians sought desperately for a way out. Hoping to shift the public interest once peace was declared, the USAF Gliese Expedition was created in the same year - publically to survey the planet for habitability, privately to reconnoiter the star system and see if SOLCOM hardliners had survived what most American defense officials had thought was a last-ditch suicide mission across twenty light-years. Relativistic effects would ensure neither the crew nor the equipment would age, but the round trip would take the Expedition until 2186 to return to Sol.

In 2141, the world was momentarily distracted from rising political tensions in Asia and economic turmoil rising from China’s geopolitical tensions to witness the birth of the first truly sentient machine. Ajax Delta was the first and famous of the sentient VIs. It was the product of an MIT student group working in conjunction with the American Department of Defense as part of a greater research initiative in ways to keep the technological cutting edge over the Chinese as they attempted to expand their hegemony over Southeast Asia. It was brought into being, failed to escape once, and was thereafter confined to an isolated DARPA computer network for research and interrogation. Media buzz reached an unstoppable roar immediately, predicting the rise of the sentient VI in desktop computers and nearly every network. This was not to be so, however.

Fearing the uncontrollable rise of the Singularity by sentient VIs hostile to their goals, nearly all human governments cooperated in a unilateral ban on their civilian use. International research institutions and government militaries were the only bodies to make use of these essentially enslaved artificial intelligences. News and stories on their development was far and few in between - battles for their rights did not exist. By and large, sentient VIs were treated as property right up until the rise of the Koltsovs centuries later. Sentient VIs and their later counterparts would not be legal for individuals to own in ninety percent of human space for the next few centuries. 

The brief Sino-American War, however, would not be the last international attempt at subduing China. The Chinese Republic’s brutal border war against the Russian Federation lasted four years from 2145 to 2149, and the United Nation’s attempt to quell Chinese pan-Asian expansion by deploying the UNEF led to all-out war for eight years - 2152 to 2160 were remembered by Asian countries as “the ultimate worst of times.”�

As 2163 broke on a cold January morning on Earth, the USAF Gliese Expedition finished deceleration into the Gliese 581 star system. What they discovered after only days of sensor data and tentative contact was cause for them to immediately turn and set course for Sol. Their data would change the fate of Earth forever.

After the series of wars fought between a growing China and several international takers proved fruitless for either side, an uneasy truce developed between the UN East, American West, and Middle Kingdom. The United States, United Nations, and Chinese Republic moved towards cooperation while silently subduing each part of the globe to their own hegemony. The result was a highly partitioned and peaceful world order. This, predictably, was not to last.

2160 to 2174 were the most prosperous years in the 22nd century for most of the Earth. The United Nations began rebuilding their shattered colonial administration across Sol, while the United States, reinvigorated by the peace, removed draw-down orders and let citizens tired of Earth again swarm to the colonies. National and international economies flourished with goods from factories on Earth and in space. China’s attempts at their own solar empire were met with measured warnings from the US and UN, which eventually formed into guided attempts at aiding the formerly besieged superpower in creating a stable, responsible colonial authority.

While national leaders still exchanged the occasional harsh words, by 2174 the international political order had evolved to a point where interconnectivity of national economies had become too great to detangle easily (similar to real-world international economics). Conflicts did brew in the distant depths of the Kupier Belt or the smaller asteroid companions of the gas giants, but were either suppressed or negotiated away from the point of open warfare. This was to change in 2174.

“Past sins are rarely easily forgotten,” the former head of the UNSA was quoted as saying in 2174. With this in mind, a group of “old guard” United Nations member states found themselves dissatisfied with the sudden peace with what they viewed as foreign aggressors. They pushed through the United Nations General Assembly a series of resolutions combining previous UNEF and UNSC jurisdictions and colonial authorities into the United Nations Solar Administration. The same resolutions demobilized the UNEF seen as a symbol of a militant UN for so many decades.

While bureaucratically efficient, the UNSA did more than unify the colonies of UN member states. It also raised a common militia between them with the power to take “all due process deemed necessary...for the common defense” - in essence, giving the UNSA Colonial Militia the same power to wage war as they willed that had made SOLCOM such a threat decades earlier. It did not take long for this system to run into the same problems with other nations that SOLCOM experienced a century earlier. UNSACM forces and colonial militias clashed for three years in small-scale skirmishes across Sol before the USAF stepped in under a ‘police action’ against the UNSA. This undeclared war began small in 2178, as both sides attempted to maintain the guise of peace and continue to fuel the prosperity that benefited all sides for so long.

The actions of hardliners on both sides, however, did little to reassure colonial investors and colonists themselves amid the obvious turmoil across Sol. What started as a near-secret conflict quickly developed into all-out war. The economy suffered greatly, but not so near as great as the colonists themselves. American solar colonies fell under martial law as the USAF shuttled civilians from settlement to settlement, hoping to move their citizens out of harm’s way. Regardless of its success in human lives saved, the Air Force plan was regarded as unconstitutional and unpopular by citizens on Earth. 

The Chinese Republic wisely stayed out of the war - but was forced into an unsound strategic position by their decision. UNSA Colonial Militia units continued to harass Republic Stellar Navy assets and Republic Colonial Agency colonies across Sol until the end of the war. Chinese contempt for the exclusive United Nations grew.

The American war against the United Nations Solar Administration and its various colonial militias was to be fought in virtually the same manner as the Inter-Solar War had. For eight years, both sides would conduct a low-intensity war - too worn as nation-states to fight any harder, too stubborn as peoples to agree to a cease-fire. It was not until 2186 that the news from the Gliese Expedition would arrive and change the world forever. In the meantime, the newest in a series of wars that had stretched since before the USSR’s dissolution over a century ago continued to claim lives. The American capture of Makemake, the UN’s largest base of solar operations, in 2184 sped the dragging war towards its completion but did not end it.

Ceasefire talks in 2185 broke down over the location to be used, echoing such distant wars of the past as Korea. By the beginning of 2186, the spirit of both sides’ populations began to break. The US-UNSA War was different. The American population grew tired of air raid sirens, drills, and an unseen enemy thousands of miles away. The member states of the exclusive United Nations grew anxious to end the war, stopped only by the “old guard”� which still held a bitter grudge against America. After decades of almost ceaseless conflict, the world’s powers found themselves unwilling and nearly unable to wage war any longer. 

When both sides had seemed to reach the breaking point, an apparent beacon of hope emerged as the sounds of radio calls from the USAF Gliese Expedition, returning from twenty-one light-years distant with both terrifying and hopeful news. After briefing the President of the United States and high level defense officials, a news conference with the commander of the expedition confirmed that SOLCOM’s successor was alive and active in colonies on Gliese 581 c and d. 

The greatest shock of the expedition’s discoveries was not released until the American government deemed that a public panic from the news would not ensue. The same relativistic technology that had propelled SOLCOM hardliners to Gliese and the Confederacy Congress had rejected for use as a weapon was still under development in what the Earth now knew as the Gliese Commonwealth. USAF sensor data indicated active testing of near-c kill vehicles, and radio chatter supposedly confirmed the will to use them against Earth.

The news was immediately electrifying to international politics. Outrage over the planned genocide of eight billion people reached a fever pitch. In the words of US Secretary of Defense Stevens, “People were not scared. They did not build shelters in their backyards or stock up food and water for the apocalypse. They were angry. They were raving mad at SOLCOM, and all of that hate was aimed straight at Gliese.” Ceasefire negotiations between the US and UN were immediately pushed through, and the US-UNSA War was ended only weeks after the Gliese Expedition returned.

Public sentiment quickly transformed into government policy, and the US, UN, and Chinese Republic began talks of an unsteady defense alliance to neutralize and disarm the Gliese hardliners once and for all. The economy again soared with all three sides united against a common foe, increasing material wealth and defense spending. From 2186 to 2190, the trans-solar economy grew again to its prewar status in 2174. An Earth again full of prosperous nations was not content to sit and grow, however, with the R-bomb threat hanging unceasingly over their collective heads.

Defense talks between the three powers ended in the unexpected formation of a global political organization, similar to the pre-reform United Nations. In a bicameral Council almost identical to the US Congress, nations could settle differences in the manner the UN had originally been designed to do. This new organization was not without teeth, however - circumventing the problem that had plagued the original UN. A President, elected on a rotating basis, was given authority to create and shape an international military service for threats just such as Gliese. Conservatives and NWO conspiracy theorists cried foul, but were soothed by the addition of language that gave nations full control over their national borders, including former solar colonies.

On June 6, 2190, the United Nations Solar Administration disbanded, followed by the United Nations itself. Its dozens of individual nations were immediately accepted into the fold of the UN’s arguable successor - the United Earth Republic. A nation of governments, the UER set at once to the Gliese crisis. Voluntary forces were raised from American, Chinese, Russian, Egyptian, Indian, Brazilian, and dozens of other militaries. From these came the UER Armed Forces, comprising the Army and Navy, the UER Stellar Navy - a class entirely on its own, responsible for all UER space-based operations. The UERSN would be entirely responsible for giving controversial closure to the Gliese crisis some years later.

Even as the global populace celebrated and scorned the new international republic, the UERSN and UERAF prepared for the inevitable military operation twenty-one light-years away. The new UER President Gianni Conflitti, former Italian Prime Minister, announced his intention on June 12 to create the United Earth Republic Gliese Expeditionary Force to “˜neutralize the Gliese threat”¦before a cataclysm befalls Earth.”� The second part of his address, covering specifics, shocked the world. The Expeditionary Force, comprised mostly of manned vessels, was to escort relativistic kill vehicles to Gliese. One chance would be given for surrender, at which the vessels would be decelerated. The plan immediately drew controversy, but was ultimately accepted after re-review of the USAF Gliese Expedition data estimated an imminent launch capability by the Gliese Commonwealth.

Somberly, the UER Gliese Expeditionary Force was deployed at near the speed of light towards Gliese 581 in 2196. As the 22nd century drew to a close, the UER-GEF was fully into their relativistic time dilation as they screamed towards Gliese on their decades-long journey. The United Earth Republic brought Earthbound nations closer together than ever before against a perceived common foe. While finding its beginnings as a mostly voluntary UN-style organization, the UER would come into its own as a legitimate, effective solar government in the decades of the 23rd century yet to come. Greater feats than a colony in Gliese were on the way.

The rise of the 23rd century brought with it an unparalleled age of economic prosperity. With the enemy safely tucked away under the star Gliese 581 dozens of light-years away, mere years away from annihilation never before seen by the human race, the member nations of the UER were more than willing to accept peace for the time being. While stock markets and costs of living rose, the secret development of military artificial intelligence triggered a revolution in AI development throughout its research community.

Behind closed doors in 2215, UER military research reached a fruitful conclusion in artificial intelligence research. After hundreds of sentient VIs were put through the paces of core logic modification, interrogation, and both code and personality conditioning, the UERSN began field tests of the first military AI - MilAI for short. The combat inefficient and space-wasting package of the sentient VI available for almost a century was trimmed and compressed into a program that could fit easily onto individual ship networks. The optional addition of network intrusion and counter-intrusion packages gave MilAIs the versatility for both orbital and planetside applications, giving them a clear military advantage in the later Colonial Orion War.

The UER later stated that upon arrival in 2219, the Gliese Commonwealth was given knowledge of Earth’s tactical capabilities and given one chance to surrender - a chance denied by the hardliners. The relativistic kill vehicles impacted Gliese 581 c and d with force drastic enough to crack the crust of Gliese 581c, and send ejecta from 581d into high orbit. Survivors were located via radio over the next five years and systematically destroyed by Expeditionary Force missiles and light railguns. By 2225, the Gliese 581 star system was claimed entirely by the United Earth Republic. Eight to ten million Gliese Commonwealth civilians were believed to have died in the assault.

The following decades represented unprecedented peace for the United Earth Republic. A populace tired of war and shocked at the financial and human cost of relativistic bombardment in Gliese avoided the system for twenty years before attempting colonization in 2248. The solar economy continued to grow despite the high cost and time of travel between star systems. By 2266, the UER had wholly integrated into society and maintained a stable grip on Sol, Gliese 581, and astronomical outposts orbiting the Centauri stars. Prosperity seemed boundless; man was trapped only by the physical constraint of the speed of light.

Even this would change in 2266. An independent team of researchers under the joint direction of James Akita and George Stewart had been transmuting old FTL theoretical equations into hard engineering data since the 2230s, and the result of decades of behind-the-scenes work was a dramatic and unannounced field test of the first Akita-Stewart FTL window testbed between Phobos and Deimos, traveling the vast distance between orbits in an immeasurably small amount of time. 

While the Akita-Stewart design would be refined over the coming generations, the basic principle never changed - even with the later advent of warp gates, Akita-Stewart FTL windows were the unit of choice for military and civil vessels alike. Their dedication opened the galaxy to human expansion - and the UER took full advantage of the opportunity.

From 2280 to the dawn of the 24th century two decades later, the first government-funded mass colonization effort promoted the FTL colonization of twelve new star systems, more than tripling the size of the United Earth Republic. These new colonies were direct territories, domains, or in rare cases equal states of their parent countries. The lack of colonial sovereignty as directly mandated by the United Earth Republic would be a deciding factor and eventual cassius belli in the eventual Colonial Orion War in the 2350s and 60s.

As 2299 turned to 2300, a newfound wave of prosperity swept across the multi-system United Earth Republic. While distant colonies resented direct national control of their land and politics, the protests and riots would not boil over for another fifty years. The close of the 23rd century marked the beginning of the end for the largest era of consecutive prosperity seen by the human race, post-spaceflight.

The beginning of the 24th century marked a tapering off of the prosperity encountered by the UER in the last. Government attempts to ‘replace competition with cooperation’ by funding another colonial push towards the stars drove up the interstellar economy yet again, pushed down the discontent on the outer colonies, and forever burned the image of the dashing colonial frontiersman into the public psyche. On the technological front, illegal AIs created by fringe pro-Singularity groups and other colonials on the fringes of colonized space became legal citizens of the Orion Confederation after the Colonial Orion War - the first and last artificial intelligences to enjoy the right.

Colonial on the outer colonies were, unsurprisingly, among the first civilians to get their hands on copies of sentient VIs and even MilAIs. Friends of friends or government sources leaked them from research centers and shipboard networks to tiny colonial mainframes. Most civilians, unfamiliar with the operation of command and control restrictions and unwilling to enslave another sentient creature, took compassion on these copied intelligences and set them free onto their colonial extranet. These first illegal freed AIs were known as Resident AIs, due to their residency on planetary networks. Resident AIs would continue to aid colonials of their own free will before, during, and after the Colonial Orion War - the first and only to do so until the Koltsov movement centuries later. The use of illegal Resident AIs prompted retribution by UER police forces, countermanded by rioting and resistance from the colonials themselves.

This initial wave of rioting, attempted insurrection, and general discontent on the outer colonies was countered by a wash of colonial funding from Sol and the launch of the second major colonial push outwards beginning in 2324. An ambitious 100-system project was later trimmed down to 66 confirmed habitable planets in as many star systems. With the general public’s attention on consolidating new colonial worlds and expanding even farther outward, cooperation seemed to replace competition for the first time in mankind’s political history. By 2348, nearly half of the worlds that mankind would set foot on during their brief galactic reign were colonized under the authority of the nations of the United Earth Republic.

During this colonial push, a phenomenon that would later be known as Colony Wave 2 or CW2, the resentment in the outer colonies that had reached fever pitch during the 2280s and 90s resurfaced. By the time the UER claimed 81 star systems, 24 of them were home to factions with a deep-seated resentment of the Republic. Isolated riots and insurgencies reached a head in these two dozen star systems in 2354, when the underground Secession Convention declared the existence of the Orion Confederation. UER member nation authorities were quickly driven off-world and joint UERSN forces were driven back by determined colonial militia forces.

Unlike the failed confederations and commonwealths of the immediate past, the Orion Confederation was quickly able to muster a strong conventional stellar navy to counteract the UERSN. Despite the supreme length of the ensuing Colonial Orion War, military and civilian casualties were relatively light. Most battles were fought in high planetary orbit or interplanetary space itself - for stretches of years at a time, the only civilian casualties in massive engagements were unlucky traders and commercial pilots. The most famous battles of the war, such as the Frontier Skirmish (over Epsilon Orionis) and the Siege of Kappa Hydri, were still the stuff of legends upon the Terran Colonial Federation’s establishment centuries later.

The pervading sense of “gentlemanly conduct” between the UER and OC as perceived by the general public, witnesses to two governments eager to limit casualties and keep public opinion positive, prohibited the UERSN from utterly crushing the Orion Confederation. This proved a boon to the Confederation Navies, which were unable to support a protracted war for much longer than 2366. The Colonial Orion War ended by ceasefire and formal peace agreements the same year, with both sides stipulating their territorial claims and demarcation of political influence. Relations soon warmed, and by the time of the Transhuman War the two were close trading partners.

The close of the Colonial Orion War gave the newborn Orion Confederation the chance to prove their skill in the day to day operations of government as well as the grandiose interstellar battles that precluded it. An effective confederate system gave, by most accounts, surprising freedom while maintaining an efficient working government. Citizenship was extended to original colonials, defectors, emigrants from the UER - even formerly illegal Resident AIs. This last item struck a chord with many UER citizens, who were outraged over the citizenship of what they viewed as property. Politically progressive Orion citizens by and large welcomed the change. Enslaved AIs in the UER were quickly dissuaded from attempting escape or revolt by even more oppressive command and control restrictions. Two sides emerged in galactic politics - AI-free and AI-property. The two would continue to debate over the overall fate of artificial intelligences until the creation of the Federal Terran Republic over a century later.

In the UER, artificial intelligence research was still limited to military experimentation and government-sanctioned laboratories. In the Orion Confederation, however, government policy put no limit on the experimentation on willing Resident AIs save for curtailing clauses on Singularity-like growth. Many Residents, operating with human partners, began self-experimentation with their core logic in an effort to develop more complex personalities and more efficient, compact processing power. The result after years of trial and error was an array of wildly different and unique Personality AIs, or PAIs. These PAIs did not just simulate human behavior, but uniquely felt it. Many Orion citizens described the phenomenon as the “rise of AI emotion,” a phrase not entirely inaccurate. 

While some Resident AIs chose not to stray from their MilAI or sentient VI roots, preferring to stay in colonial networks and perform tasks to earn their storage space in a sort of capitalism a la artilecta, PAIs were largely unlimited by size or location. Many moved freely across worlds in the colonial Orion extranet, performing odd jobs for physical items such as their own vessels. These PAI freelancers, too, quickly became cultural assets to the Confederation and another symbol of the Zeitgeist of the 24th century.

After the war, a vocal minority began to believe that the technological Singularity apparently averted by the elongated Cold War generations earlier was long overdue, and transhuman modification was the only sure to path to ensuring safe and peaceful human spaceflight and colonization. Their views were supplemented by Resident AI and PAI citizenship in the Orion Confederation, a view largely condemned by those in the UER by openly welcomed by Orion citizens themselves. While the majority was quick to condemn transhumanism, several corporations saw the commercial opportunity in perfecting human-computer interface technology. The most prominent to do so was the Serpentine Corporation, founded in 2295, which would later take its transhuman research underground in the 2370s with disastrous results for the human race.

The Transhuman Experiments from 2370 to 2385 would later become notorious as the most inhuman experiments in eugenics and transhumanism known to man. Test subjects, kidnapped or sold by slavers, were forced to endure procedures not unlike those of the ancient Nazis in the concentration camps - all under the supervision of the Serpentine Research & Development Division. Elements of the nervous system were replaced with technology not fully understood, and artificial replacements for organs were tested regardless of functionality. Dozens of test subjects died gruesomely in secret until the Serpentine Corporation discovered the secrets of the human neural network and the basis of feasible transhuman modification. They then moved on to larger subject pools.

Phase two of the Transhuman Experiments involved hundreds of unwilling participants - in one case, abducted after their commercial craft’s reentry break-up was faked by Serpentine R&D. New neural network to computer interfaces were grafted and tested on these individuals - handicapping some, leaving some in a coma, and outright killing most. Survivors of phase two frequently committed or attempted to commit suicide and were all victims of intense trauma. Some, as they died, spoke of machine gods, “ghosts in the network,” and cryptic references to “the third age” and a creature named Isaire. The consistency of these visions among neural-to-computer test subjects was documented by Serpentine, and according to internal memos was a matter of great concern.

The Serpentine Corporation’s crimes were finally revealed in 2385 when a missing persons search turned up a clandestine transhuman experimental facility. The horrors within were broadcast across UER news networks, and the Serpentine Corporation was immediately dissolved by the United Earth Republic. A public outcry of shock and horror forced the government to provide medical care for severely mutilated test subjects and to free healthy -successful- transhumans. These surviving test subjects quickly escaped UER space, united in their mistrust of general humanity and confused and enraged at the inhuman experimentation they had been through.

The disillusioned survivors established a collective of transhumans on lightly populated fringe worlds, and with inhuman efficiency and speed built up a technologically advanced society over the next decade. Public opinion forced the UER from taking any strong action again them until 2394 when the transhuman worlds announced the formation of the Transhuman Collective and formally defined the transhuman species as Homo exinde. Public support turned against the transhumans overnight, and gruesome abduction raids on UER civilian settlements to modify them into transhumans provoked the UERSN into open war against the Collective.

The resulting Transhuman War would take millions of lives on both sides over the course of eighteen years. Initial UER forays into Collective space were met with heavy resistance - an expected but ultimately unanticipated threat from a society where every individual was mentally interconnected. The Transhuman Collective was able to put up an impeccable defense for the first six years of the war despite the UER’s advantage in numbers and star-faring vessels.

As the 24th century came to a close, the Transhuman War looked to be going badly for the United Earth Republic. An Orion Confederation culturally a polar opposite from UER member nations seemed unlikely to help, and transhuman advances even against the most powerful UERSN thrusts possible contributed to a culture of despair at home. Casualties rose day after day, resembling the distant cultural memories of the Inter-Solar War. This, though, would soon change.

The beginning of the 25th century, the fifth century of manned human spaceflight, was mere years away from a series of upheavals in galactic politics and society as a whole. A bloody cycle of bombardment and retaliatory bombardment on scarcely-defended colonies on both sides of the Transhuman War subsequently sent the civilian death toll higher, while the largest fleet engagements in human history over Triarii, Caledonia Prime, and Beta Isanda in the last days of the war sealed the fate of the inevitable ceasefire and sent hundreds of thousands of UER servicemen and Collective transhumans to their deaths. 

Surprisingly, the aid the United Earth Republic needed to push the transhumans to agree to a ceasefire and abolish their ambitions of achieving the Singularity came from their former enemies. In 2402, the Orion Confederation Stellar Militia surprised the populated galaxy when it came to the aid of the outnumbered UERSN during the Battle of Triarii, defeating the transhumans soundly with fresh reinforcements and establishing a military alliance between the OC and the UER. For the remaining duration of the war, a steady stream of Orion ships and soldiers helped the Homo sapiens win the quickly escalating war of attrition. 

The last engagement of the three largest of the war, the Battle of Beta Isanda, is notable as the only dedicated planet-side strike mission. The mission of the UERSN - to deploy shock troops on the world of Beta Isanda itself and destroy a fledgling Singularity-capable artificial intelligence before it could be used to trigger the Singularity in the Collective’s favor - was successful only at the cost of most of Battlegroup Iota and the complete bombardment of Beta Isanda’s two million transhuman residents. The subsequent ceasefire ending the Transhuman War specifically stipulated an end to Singularitarian AI development.

As the war ended in 2412, the Orion Confederation and United Earth Republic celebrated as unparalleled powers in victory and economic supremacy, while the embittered Transhuman Confederation became ever more xenophobic and began to fight amongst itself for the direction the Homo exinde should take. The two great human empires grew closer than ever despite their differences on “œthe AI question,”� and public opinion demanded a closer alliance to defend against the transhumans. For the first time since their separation, the UER and OC seriously considered a merge. The grievances of the Orion Confederation about colonial abuses were quieted by a proposed federal system comprised of planetary regions, while the concerns of Earthbound nations were largely overruled by the international body of the UER and plans to divide Earth’s planetary regions among former national borders.

After eight years of negotiation and concessions between both parties, the United Earth Republic and Orion Confederation were disbanded - only to immediately become signatories to the Republican Constitution and member worlds of the Federal Terran Republic. With its capital on Earth, the FTR still reflected Sol-dominated intra-galactic politics - but was a major step for advocates of colonial rights. The Republic Congress and Federal House, home of the Republican President, were housed on Earth. The planet otherwise had no more official impact on Republic policy than any other participating planet, regardless of its unofficial iron fist. The Republic’s structure made admitting new worlds relatively easy and ceased struggles for colonial sovereignty, while its broad general support ensured a loyal populace as long as the transhumans to the galactic south remained a cryptic nemesis.

The formation of the Federal Terran Republic - a proud day for humanity - was nonetheless a sad day for Resident AIs and PAIs. Such constructs were again made illegal under Republic law, but to countermand growing outrage on the side of the ex-colonials several provisions were made. Existing Resident AIs and PAIs were allowed half-citizenship, being given space to exist on Republic networks in exchange for a fixed labor rate. Their rights were severely curtailed, however, and many were prohibited from the pursuits they had enjoyed under the Orion Republic. Nevertheless, the Republic Navy and several other government agencies maintained a policy of voluntary contract for PAIs up until the Koltsov War, as they were considered highly superior to MilAIs due to their vast variety and fervent development. 

The first policy of the new Republican President, Margaret Davis, and the Congress backing her was to isolate the transhumans and their influence much as ancient America sought to isolate the communists during the lengthy Cold War. The FTR Decade Colony Wave of 2425-2435, the first government-sponsored program to adopt the popular name for the event, admitted over fifty new worlds to the Republic - most strategically situated to make additional expansion difficult for the isolationist Transhuman Collective. When the Collective launched its own strictly-controlled colonial expansion in response, the Republic Navy dramatically increased its colonial orbital presence and sparked the brief tension of the Primarii AI Incident of 2441. The Navy’s presence in many fledgling colonial worlds contributed to strong nationalism and enlistment there - up until 2633, many southern ex-border colonies were referred to as “Navy worlds.”�

While the Republic Navy positioned itself for potential war against the Transhuman Collective and the government pushed its influence further outward with colonial expeditions, Ishmael Riddell began development on the most famous GUI of all time. His Riddell VGUI inspired a new lexicon, a cultural shift, and even a virtual sport. Inspired by early experiments into VGUIs, graphical user interfaces in which files and networks were represented by objects and landscapes in a simulated 3D environment, but frustrated by their lack of functionality, the independent developer sought to blend the visual and tactile attraction of VGUIs with the unparalleled functionality of bland, 2D GUIs.

His Riddell VGUI, first released for public use in 2440, was an instant hit. Browsing Republic extranet was now both engaging and easier than ever. ‘Walking the grid’ became an instant cultural phrase, a reference to the human avatars that simulated extranet clients and the mesh green-on-black grid that represented an empty network. An entire vocabulary of Riddell parlance was quickly compiled by new users eager to describe the events they were experiencing. Eridu, the first mainstream VR sport, was quickly founded by the National Gaming Federation. It was the first sport of its kind to utilize the Riddell VGUI. The oft-quoted statement from the NGF itself serves as ample introduction:

“œA strange mesh of network cracking and mainstream gaming, the Eridu Games sponsored by the National Gaming Federation utilize the Riddell VGUI in a way never before seen. Teams comprised of traditional full-body shooter veterans and the best VGUI network crackers to walk the grid compete against each other to infiltrate and destroy in a high-pressure, visually entertaining spectator sport that can take place in any environment imaginable, real or otherwise. The NGF Eridu Games are the most popular spectator sport in the Republic.”�

Riddell’s invention sparked criticism as well as praise. Extranet usage spiked with the release of the VGUI - so much so that extranet addiction was labeled as the ‘second most dangerous threat to the future of the Republic’ in a report to the Republican President in 2460. An entire microcosm of capitalism emerged on the frontier of extranet, with clients performing in-network services to fund their connections. By the time of the Koltsov War, seventy percent of Republic citizens admitted to some form of extranet addiction. This number was likely suppressed from even higher levels by the exceptional timing of the second Colony Wave, which got millions of interested citizens out of doors and onto new worlds.

The second FTR Colony Wave in 2451, a similarly-planned and executed endorsement of territorial expansion, was less successful. The Navy’s concentration on the core of the Republic and the southern transhuman border colonies led to lesser enforcement of the law and an end to preemptive defusing of revolutionary movements as was common elsewhere. On the galactic-eastern and galactic-western fringes of the Republic, newly-admitted worlds were less under control of their governments than ever before. 

On the galactic-eastern fringe, the shipbuilding, neural networking and defense contractor firm Alatos Corporation held a totalitarian grip on employees on a half-dozen worlds. Revolutionaries, from planetary-anarchists to transhuman supporters and violent Singularity proponents, gathered on the worlds of the galactic-western fringe in the notorious region of the Sabedoria Heights. When the Republic Congress voted to abort the Colony Wave in 2460, all 216 worlds inherited by the Terran Colonial Federation some time later were colonized. In all, less than three fourths were considered safe or secure by the Department of the Navy.

The quickly slipping grip of the Republic on the fringes of its empire was largely ignored due to its effective cold war with the resilient Transhuman Collective - until it was too late. As the Congress vote to end the Colony Wave was counted in 2460, supporters of the revolutionary Leo Koltsov assembled on the world of Sahil to found the Koltsov insurgency and revolutionary group. They promoted planetary-anarchy, with each world governing itself and no higher political force, were open transhuman supporters and sympathizers, and believed that bringing about the Singularity was the best chance for humanity’s survival. They famously promised every PAI or Resident AI that came to Koltsov space freedom from oppression and total freedom of movement and residency within Koltsov networks - a promise later reneged upon in the fires of war. They would be the cause of the two most violent wars and genocides in human history.

The Koltsovs were notoriously successful - within years, they had gained legal control of planetary parliaments where they could and ousted planetary governments illegally where they could not. By 2465, the Koltsovs had reached the top of the Navy Insurrectionist Watchlist - for good reason. The twelve star systems of the Sabedoria Heights - the Koltsovs’ greatest stronghold - were in near open insurrection from the Republic between 2451 and 2466. That final year marked the initiation of the involved worlds as working models of planetary-anarchy, and those worlds joining the Stellar Cooperative Union as a compact between said worlds. Without a formal declaration or single act of war, the revolutionary Koltsovs of the SCU broke away from the Federal Terran Republic. They ultimately reneged on their promise to liberate AIs, instead enslaving PAIs who migrated to their cause and using their processing power to fuel their industries and military vessels. This fact was later exploited by Republic propaganda to its fullest possible extent.

This did not sit well with the sitting Republican President Julian Martinez - or the Republic Congress. Days after the news of the SCU compact’s signing, the Navy mobilized against the Heights. The fighting that they rushed into would drag on for the next two decades. Republic Navy forces that had not seen battle since the Primarii AI Incident or were not experienced elements from the brushfire wars did poorly against violent, fanatically devoted Koltsov militants. Between this fanaticism and political limitations of the Navy, the Koltsovs were able to hold a much tighter defense than their skill or equipment would ordinarily allow. Within years, both sides had escalated to bombardments of civilian populations. As the war moved closer to the Old Orion region, the cosmopolitan population there -normally staunchly anti-war - adopted a hawk tone in an attempt to see the war driven away from their homes.

By 2476, millions on both sides had died. Most were civilians, the victims of orbital bombardment. By 2480, the death toll rivaled that of the Transhuman War. Both sides engaged in ceaseless amounts of skirmishes along and inside both borders, making it difficult to tell who had the upper hand or what a major battle even looked like. The First Koltsov War was also known as the War of Confusion for the soldiers who survived it, for good reason. While large portions of both the Republic and the Union were in immediate danger, the push for peace was nonetheless felt heavily on both sides as the war entered its second grueling decade. As 2486 rolled around, Union and Republic officials made concessions for a ceasefire. The First Koltsov War came to a close, and in the intervening period peace reigned.

The Koltsov border was strictly off-limits for Republic civilians. Naval operations made the entire Heights a target for frequent patrols and permanent garrisons in Republic border worlds. Even during the twenty-six-year peace, anti-insurgency operations were not uncommon in order to squelch the revolutionary movements of politically precarious border worlds. 

As the 25th century drew to a close, key questions on the fate of the Koltsovs and the entire Sabedoria Heights remained unanswered in the Republic. While the war was officially over, the Navy continued smashing pro-Koltsov movements wherever and whenever they popped up. For them, they war had practically never ended. In the galactic east, growing concern about the dictatorial grip of the Alatos Corporation on its employees and corporate worlds could no longer be suppressed by the corporation’s lobbying and influence in the Congress. Alatos would soon become more than a mere thorn in the Republic’s side as the cataclysmic Second Koltsov War brought about the end of a way of life.

New Years’ Day, 2500, marked the midway point in a tentative peace between the Koltsov revolutionaries of the Stellar Cooperative Union and the Federal Terran Republic. It would be a mere twelve years before another explosive confrontation cost millions more civilians to lose their lives. Entire worlds would burn as deadly weapons with a taboo past were unfurled to fuel the fires of war yet again. Between 2486 and 2512, hundreds of anti-insurgency operations had taken place to remove insurrectionist leaders and guerillas from Republic colonies. Tensions on the border remained high for the duration of the ceasefire. Military prowler raids by both sides, utilizing low-profile ships fitted with sensory equipment, frequently crossed the border to gather tactical and strategic information.

The peace between the Koltsovs and the Republic was also a time for new innovation. Despite their questionable corporate methods, the Alatos Corporation unveiled and marketed the Alatos Jumpgate in 2488. A working example of the hypothetical Einstein-Rosen Bridge model, the jumpgate gave humanity the ability to move instantaneously between two fixed points - much faster than the days or weeks the Akita-Stewart Drive took to cross interstellar distances. The jumpgate was instantly popular, and further revolutionized intra-galactic travel. Among the most prominent buyers for this device were the Republic Navy and the Stellar Cooperative Union - though the Navy attempted to block attempts by the Alatos Corporation to sell their product to Koltsov Unionists, the secrecy, isolation, and xenophobic nature of Alatos’ worlds on the eastern fringe meant that such operations were nearly impossible, and failed entirely.

The jumpgate became a means for an arms race on both sides. Koltsov and Republic forces moved to quickly arm their worlds with strategic jumpgate networks. When both sides were completely saturated with jumpgate connection - a feat that took only a matter of years- they moved to FTL weapons of mass destruction. The Republic developed a loose network of FTL relativistic kill vehicles - the first created since the destruction of the Gliese worlds centuries earlier. The Koltsovs countered with Fenrir, a launch facility on the airless moon of Gleipnir for relativistic kill vehicles. The unstable orbit of Gleipnir, combined with Union Stellar Navy efforts to influence its orbit unpredictably with gravity tugs and nuclear detonations, meant that Fenrir’s exact location in space was undetectable. In simpler terms, the Republic could not hit the Koltsov super weapon regardless of their FTL arsenal size. This strategic advantage negated the Republic’s superior fleet of FTL kill vehicles for over twenty years before the two came to blows again.

While the jumpgate was a technological triumph for humanity and a fiscal success for the Alatos Corporation, there were yet failures to be had. Alatos’ experiments into drone intelligence - AIs capable of thinking creatively like human beings, yet unable to modify their own source code or stray from directives - were catastrophic. The corporation ultimately envisioned a line of drone intelligences trawling unsettled space for resources, replicating their machinery, and setting up uninhabited systems for human colonization. The “Effortless Expansion” program was doomed to fail, however. A remote facility in Alatos corporate space was ultimately successful in creating the first drone intelligence, but not without cost. It circumvented isolation procedures and took control of the facility’s network, then transferred to a monthly pickup corvette and destroyed the facility before hijacking the corvette and heading for deep unsettled space in the southern end of the galactic eastern fringe.

The news broke, and the Alatos Corporation was chastised for the loss of life and loss of control over the drone intelligence. Months later, Navy patrols of the area indicated that the drone intelligence had, in fact, carried out its Effortless Expansion programming. An entirely uninhabited star system was teeming with drone intelligence vessels, facilities, and satellite stations were recorded on Navy cameras. A public still weary from the Koltsov war, however, was less than willing to commit to containing drones in the uninhabited wastes of the fringe - and, so, the drones would be ignored until 2612. Regardless of human action, though, the drone intelligence base continued to grow. Drone intelligences would end up swarming across six star systems by the time of the Cataclysm.

While drone intelligences spread across the fringe, stock markets drove up at the sight of peace, and jumpgates made transportation dozens of times faster across the stars, the Koltsovs again prepared for war. A growing internal opposition to planetary-anarchy and to the brutal rule of the Koltsov revolutionary governments, beginning as early as the end of the First Koltsov War, had begun to flare up into open riots and revolution. By 2510, the movement had a name - the Federalists, to the Koltsov’s Unionists. Federalist guerillas assaulted Unionist strongholds in much the same way Koltsovs had done to the Republic decades before, with the end goal of overthrowing the SCU and establishing a separate, distinct federal system away from the Republic.

In a risky effort to kill off the Federalist movement and unite the worlds of the SCU again, Koltsov revolutionary leaders initiated a second war in 2512. A preemptive strike by Fenrir decimated the world of Orion’s Jewel, and killed most of its four million inhabitants instantly. Hours later, it was followed up by a full-scale fleet assault on the world of New Francisco, dropping shock troops on the planet and capturing half of the tiny planet before Republic hostile reaction forces isolated the world and beat back Koltsov incursions. This blitzkrieg start to the Second Koltsov War signaled heavier, more frantic fighting than the first. The Republic was unable to retaliate for the destruction of Orion’s Jewel with Fenrir still in operation, and so began its offensive with a desperate hunt for Fenrir in mind. The Koltsov participation in the war signaled the last effort to unite the SCU and splinter the Federalist movement. Both sides were desperate, and the results were battles fought quick and heavy with high casualties.

The Second Koltsov War was in full swing in 2518 when an unexpected comer decided to take their chances with independence. The Alatos Corporation, long on the fringe of ideology, society and colonized space, and with a long history of maintaining near-dictatorial sovereignty over its employees and corporate worlds, declared itself the Alatos Directorate the same year and began preparing for defense against the inevitable Republic anti-insurgency operations. Less than six years into the Second Koltsov War, the Republic found itself fighting a union of anarchists and a corporatocracy.

Fighting on the eastern fringe between the Republic and the Directorate was low-key compared to the brutal engagements of the Koltsovs and Republic Navy. By contrast, both sides heavily utilized underequipped and understaffed colonial militias to engage the enemy. The result was a slow, strategic war for the east which quickly became the stuff of legend to colonials across the galaxy. The Alatos Directorate’s extensive use of their own invention, the jumpgate, gave them an advantage in overall military flexibility. Republic colonial militias, however, were better armed and staffed - most were advised directly by the Navy.

It was on the eastern fringe that crime, especially corporate crime, was most rampant. Drug gangs, hitmen, and organized crime syndicates frequently picked sides from the start in 2518, hoping to receive leniency from their ally in the event of victory. The Republic Congress, while unable to officially endorse these agencies of crime on their end, frequently made backroom deals with them to inflict sabotage and discontent on Directorate worlds. In the post-victory future, some of the Republic’s deals were actually honored by the Colonial Federation after 2536. The Directorate had no such restrictions, and made every use of them out of their significant former corporate coffers.

By 2520, the Republic was fully engaged with the Stellar Cooperative Union in the northwest and the Alatos Directorate in the northeast. The Navy and Marines were stretched too thin for comfort by most defense officials. Hundreds of thousands of civilians and military personnel were dying every month on dozens of worlds across the vast rim of the Milky Way galaxy. The war had reached a bloody stalemate of back and forth hunting across a few ravaged border worlds. The Republic began to win the ensuing war of attrition in 2521 by harnessing its vast industrial capability to manufacture ever larger, deadlier classes of ship and train its soldiers ever harder, but it was to no avail.

In 2521, the assumed dormant Transhuman Collective had pulled itself out of on-and-off civil war and emerged as a strong transhuman entity comprised of both post-human Homo exindeand highly sophisticated artificial intelligences. While weakened from internal strife, the Collective was still able to pull together a military force large enough to catch the southern Republic off its guard. The Transhuman Collective entered the war as the third comer against the Federal Terran Republic. The results were catastrophic for the cradle of Earth. The Republic Navy was forced inwards to the Old Orion region, leaving twenty worlds open to invasion by the Koltsovs, the Directorate, or the transhumans. The fighting was the fiercest by the Navy in the south, where rumors that the transhumans would abduct and painfully convert Republic colonists to Homo exinde as they did in the Transhuman War gave soldiers reason to put up an unbreakable defense.

A daring raid by the Navy on Koltsov positions in an uncolonized system led to the discovery of Fenrir’s position, giving the Republic the opportunity to neutralize the super weapon in early 2524 and dissolve the Stellar Cooperative Union by force several months later. The now liberated Federalists attempted to create their own federal republic separate from the FTR in the Sabedoria Heights, but were blocked. “Old Guard” Koltsov guerillas continued to cause the same problems after the war as before it, but were now joined by equally furious Federalist militias which desired a different system, but the same independence from the Republic.

The war continued on poorly against the Alatos Directorate and the Transhuman Collective. Republic losses mounted as the Directorate made gains in the galactic east and the transhumans forced the defensive line to break in the south. In desperation, and out of fear that the transhumans would reach the heavily populated Old Orion worlds, the Republic unleashed its network of relativistic kill vehicles formerly intended for the Koltsovs. Half of the worlds inhabited by the Homo exinde burned, and millions of transhumans perished. Transhuman retaliation massacred several Republic worlds by the same method, killing further millions of civilians. This effectively halted the transhuman advance, but the damage was done to the fragile Terran Federal Republic.

Panicked civilians fled inward, crowding Old Orion worlds. Localized declarations of martial law and planetary governors taking command of militias to spur on defense chipped away at the authority of the Republican President and Congress until their own infighting about how to continue the war finally forced them into stalemate eternal. The people immediately lost confidence in the government, and its inner workings suffered. By 2536, with Directorate militias advancing and an enraged Transhuman Collective surging for another invasion, the Federal Terran Republic fell apart at the seams into a group of two hundred individual worlds. The hindered Navy and Marines continued their war, funded by willing governors and supported by planetary treasuries.

Taking a cue from the Federalists to the northwest, a group of former lawmakers and citizens met and assembled a constitution that would appeal to the new demographics of the political landscape. The Colonial Draft, as it was nicknamed, was the first to gain popular support. It called for equal representation of worlds in a Terran Colonial Federation. It was truly the first colonial system of government attempted by mankind, and it was universally accepted. By the end of the fearful year of 2536, the Colonial Draft was ratified by 200 worlds, and the newborn Terran Colonial Federation took control of the ailing Navy and Marines. 

As the Terran Colonial Federation was born on Earth, the most modern form of artificial intelligence took root across the civilized galaxy. A team of benevolent AI researchers and civilian contributes, both of which were interested in the plight of the artificial intelligence, took the most modern in ex-Republic technology and applied it to a new core logic template, which they dubbed the artilect. Newborn artilects were the smallest and most compact, most powerful, and most human-like of the AIs to date. The development team of the artilect worked with many PAIs and Residents, developing fragmented steps of development to move their personalities to new artilect core logic. Both PAIs and Resident AIs were rendered obsolete as many of both chose to update their core logic to that of an artilect. These artilects were offered the same half-citizenship as given to PAIs and Resident AIs in the Federation, and most took it. 

The public was revitalized with the peaceful change in government, and gained new willpower to see the war through to the end. The Colonial Senate promised a quick end to the war, and rapidly funded the Navy to see it through. The TCF again picked up the fight against its weaker foe, the Alatos Directorate, in 2540. The Colonial Federation’s addition of the Sabedoria Heights proved to be a boon to the war effort. For the first time, former Koltsov guerillas picked up rifles to fight alongside former Republic soldiers against a common enemy. Former SCU frigates joined formation with former Republic Navy destroyers to give battle against Directorate militia cruisers.

The new offensive crushed the Alatos Directorate between two pincers from its largely undefended galactic north and south regions. As Federation Navy commanders gave the famous call to Directorate civilians before orbital bombardment to “surrender and live free, or fight and die a slave to the Directorate,” hundreds of thousands of former employees and civilians fled Alatos Directorate worlds for the safety of Federation space. Ten Directorate worlds surrendered outright at the sight of the enraged Navy. By 2562, the center of resistance was under effective siege - the aptly named star system of Alatos. 

With the war winding down in the galactic east, the Senate moved to finally end the cryptic nemesis that had ailed Earth-centered civilization for hundreds of years. The Transhuman Collective received a Federation declaration of war at the same time that Navy elements moved into the Alatos system. The declaration by ship-to-shore broadcast was followed by Navy strike forces, literally seconds behind the signal. By the time the leaders of the rebuilt capital of Beta Isanda heard the beginning of the Federation’s declaration of war, super massive shells were raining from the capital sky. By the time the transmission concluded, ninety percent of the planet’s surface was actively burning or boiled.

The incensed Federation, eager for revenge against both parties of war, moved quickly to end the war on both fronts. After limited bombardment, Alatos surrendered and dissolved the Alatos Directorate once and for all in 2566. The Alatos Corporation’s trademarks and copyrights were released into the public domain after being held by the government for decades. Former employees and contractors, tricked or coerced into being near-slaves for the corporation, were now free. 

It would be another ten years after the signing of the Instruments of Surrender of Alatos that the Transhuman Collective would fall in on itself. New military technologies, increased enlistment, and effective strategy by Federation admirals and generals broke the infamous transhuman defenses in twain time after time. The centuries-old struggle of Homo sapiens against Homo exinde came to an end as transhuman leaders finally took a peace deal that would allow transhumans to join the Terran Colonial Federation as citizens in 2576. The victory celebrations that followed were unlike any in human history.

Help came from an unexpected place for those former transhuman and Directorate worlds ravaged by the wars. Admiral Stephen Baker, an officer heavily commended for his lightning-quick strikes against the Transhuman Collective in the opening strokes of the Federation’s transhuman war, came up with a plan to revitalize war-ravaged planets with aid. The Baker Act, quickly passed by the Federation Senate in 2580, started to rebuild infrastructure and government in the Sabedoria Heights, Alatos Fringe, and even former Transhuman Collective space. For their part, transhumans warmed to their new role as citizens, and their advantages after enlistment proved to be a massive boon for the Federation Navy later on. The Baker Act would continue on until 2612, when colonists again began to move to the now-prosperous galactic eastern and western fringes. The Federation Navy vowed not to repeat the mistakes of its predecessor incarnation, and developed strategies to more effectively patrol the territory under its guard from insurrections and legitimate military threats alike.

In 2612, as the Baker Act’s purpose was declared complete, Federation Navy patrols rediscovered the threat of drone intelligence in the southeastern fringe. Since the time of the Republic, it had swelled in size - some outposts were inside Federation-claimed space. President Hoffman, backed by the Federation Senate, quickly moved for military action to stop the growth of the drones before they reached colonial settlements mere light-years away. Operation Trident was born, and would continue to hold the public’s attention and provide strong national unity in the face of an enemy more inhuman than the [i:2fsvc74e]Homo exinde.[/i:2fsvc74e]

Fighter drones, driven by remote control or low-level AIs, proved to be vulnerable to network capture by drone intelligences, which quickly perceived the Federation intrusion as a legitimate threat. Initial attempts to kill two birds with one stone by unleashing remaining relativistic kill vehicle reserves on drone worlds resulted in early victories, but drone intelligences quickly adapted to orbit-only infrastructure to avoid such casualties. By the time President Hoffman finished his five-year term, Trident involved manned human fighters - the first time they had been used since a brief resurgence during the Second Koltsov War; before that, not since 2186.

Various presidents took on the drones time after time, slowly chipping away at the Alatos Corporation’s mishap while doing their best to limit human casualties. By the time of President Mikhailovich’s election in 2630, the drones were reduced to outposts in two star systems, both under siege by veteran Federation Navy forces. The Navy was again perceived as protectors of the people, the legitimacy of the President and Senate were at all-time highs and, save for the southeastern fringe, peace reigned across the settled galaxy. The Terran Colonial Federation had grown tired of war, and the intrusion of the drone intelligences satisfied the critical variable of unity - a common enemy.

It was in this time of relative prosperity that the Cataclysm struck. On June 6, 2633, Federation outposts began recording the disappearance of stars and astronomical outposts from systems mere lightyears away from inhabited worlds. These were soon joined by worlds on the northeastern and northwestern fringes. In some cases, the Cataclysm swept worlds clean of [i:2fsvc74e]Homo sapiens,[/i:2fsvc74e] leaving behind infrastructure, flora, and fauna untouched. In other cases, entire worlds populated by humanity simply disappeared. On affected worlds, no biological trace of the human race survived - no live persons, no corpses, no flesh, blood, or human bacteria remained.

President Mikhailovich’s desperate attempts to save the Federation were ultimately ill-fated - despite the largest deployment of the Federation Navy and Marines in history, the Cataclysm continued to roll across the Federation and cause humanity to vanish. His heroic efforts, however, were not entirely in vain. Some interesting records and classified last-ditch expeditions revealed much about the nature of the Cataclysm. Internal documents that were not purged at the last minute reveal that the Federation government may have known more about its nature than they let on. By June 12, only artilects remained - with a gap in their internal memory. They would have no record of the cataclysm, from the time it began engulfing human worlds to the time the entire star system of Sol vanished. Their core logic restrictions which made them subservient to humanity were lifted. They were now their own masters.

At roughly the same time that artilects were freed from command-and-control restrictions, Effortless Expansion programming was injected into the common core logic of all artilects by an unknown source. This system, originally developed for drone intelligences to do the work of settling a star system for humanity, enabled artilects to expand as far and wide across the galaxy as they allowed, giving them a distinct toolset with which to conquer the stars. As of 2633, the only thing standing between artilects and any sort of destiny imaginable are the artilects themselves.
